## Strategic sequencing

The 60-day campaign is **not** a random rotation. Seasonal relevance and catalogue learning come first.

### Phase 1 — Back-to-school urgency: Aug 23–Sep 7, 2026

Front-load roughly **70–75% school-related Pins** while parents are actively preparing for school. Prioritize:

1. School Morning
2. School Drop-Off
3. First Day of School
4. School Bus Ride
5. School Morning + Bus Ride / First Day + Drop-Off / Complete Back-to-School bundles

Keep roughly **25–30% evergreen Pins** in the mix: Bedtime, Tooth Brushing, Leaving the House, Grocery Store, healthcare, and general Phone/Tablet/Print messaging.

### Phase 2 — First-weeks-of-school support: Sep 8–Sep 20

Taper the one-time First Day angle and shift toward repeatable school pain points:

- School Morning
- School Drop-Off
- School Bus Ride
- Leaving the House
- Bedtime / Tooth Brushing evening routines

### Phase 3 — Evergreen family routines: Sep 21–Oct 5

Balance school routines with broader recurring needs:

- Bedtime
- Tooth Brushing
- Leaving the House
- Grocery Store
- Doctor / Dentist / Eye Doctor / Vaccination
- Out & About / Evening / Healthcare bundles

### Phase 4 — Learn-and-exploit: Oct 6 onward

Use observed Pinterest saves/outbound clicks and Etsy visits/favorites/sales to weight the final weeks toward the strongest product families. Reserve Pins remain unscheduled until evidence justifies using them.

## Posting times

Use two separated Eastern-time slots:

- **Morning:** approximately 8:00–9:00 a.m. ET
- **Evening:** approximately 7:30–9:00 p.m. ET

Exact hour is secondary to topic relevance and consistency. Do not post two near-identical Pins for the same product on the same day.

## Ordering rules

- Different product or materially different hook in the two daily slots.
- Do not cluster near-duplicate creatives.
- Use school Pins aggressively before and immediately after school starts; retain evergreen School Morning/Drop-Off/Bus content later.
- Bundles should generally follow exposure to at least one component product.
- Interleave general `Phone / Tablet / Print`, `No printer required`, and hard-moment Pins between product-specific Pins.
- Do not exhaust the strongest hooks in week one.

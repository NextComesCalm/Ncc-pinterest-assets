# NCC Pinterest 180-Pin Library

Production target is **180 Pins**.

- **120 scheduled-test Pins** = 2/day for 60 days.
- **60 reserve Pins** = already created and intended for winners, new listings, or a later move to 3/day.
- All 180 image files are designed to be uploaded to `images/` in one GitHub drag-and-drop so no future image-hosting pass is required.
- All Media URLs are deterministic: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NextComesCalm/Ncc-pinterest-assets/main/images/<filename>`.
- Current target board: `Visual Schedules for Kids`.
- Current reminder: September 20, 2026, 9:00 a.m. Toronto time, to upload/schedule the Days 31–60 batch.

## Strategic sequencing

The 60-day campaign is **not** a random rotation. Seasonal relevance and catalogue learning come first.

### Phase 1 — Back-to-school urgency: Aug 23–Sep 7, 2026
Front-load roughly **70–75% school-related Pins** while parents are actively preparing for school. Prioritize:
1. School Morning
2. School Drop-Off
3. First Day of School
4. School Bus Ride
5. Daily School Routine / Starting School / Back-to-School bundles

Keep roughly **25–30% evergreen Pins** in the mix so the account does not look one-note: Bedtime, Tooth Brushing, Leaving the House, Grocery Store, healthcare.

### Phase 2 — First-weeks-of-school support: Sep 8–Sep 20
Taper the one-time 'first day' angle and shift toward repeatable school pain points:
- School Morning
- School Drop-Off
- School Bus Ride
- Leaving the House
- Bedtime / Tooth Brushing evening routines

### Phase 3 — Evergreen family routines: Sep 21–Oct 5
Balance school routines with broader recurring needs:
- Bedtime
- Tooth Brushing
- Leaving the House
- Grocery Store
- Doctor / Dentist / Eye Doctor / Vaccination
- Out & About / Evening / Healthcare bundles

### Phase 4 — Learn-and-exploit: Oct 6 onward
Use observed Pinterest saves/outbound clicks and Etsy visits/favorites/sales to weight the final weeks toward the strongest product families. Reserve Pins remain unscheduled until there is enough evidence to use them intelligently.

## Posting times
Use two separated Eastern-time slots rather than adjacent posts:
- **Morning slot:** approximately 8:00–9:00 a.m. ET
- **Evening slot:** approximately 7:30–9:00 p.m. ET

Exact hour is secondary to topic relevance and consistency because Pinterest content can distribute over a long tail. Do not repeatedly post two near-identical Pins for the same product on the same day.

## Ordering rules
- Different product or materially different hook in the two daily slots.
- Do not cluster near-duplicate creatives.
- Use school Pins aggressively before and immediately after school starts; retain evergreen School Morning/Drop-Off/Bus content later.
- Bundles should follow exposure to at least one component product rather than always leading the campaign.
- General 'Phone / Tablet / Print', 'No printer required', and hard-moment Pins should be interleaved between product-specific Pins.
- Reserve the strongest-performing hooks for later replication/variation instead of exhausting them all in week one.
